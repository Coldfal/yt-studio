# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. Be constructive, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be handled promptly and discreetly. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.
